          Ex 7

          Talk about your day.

          Spend a few minutes thinking about what to say. Record your answer. Play to hear your answer.
          Talk about:
          • when you wake up and get up
          • what you have for breakfast
          • how you go to school or work
          • what you do in the evenings, and when you go to bed
          • how often you do things: always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never, once a week, twice a day…
          💡 Example: I usually wake up at 6.30, but I get up at 6.45. I always have breakfast with my family. I go to school by bus. I play football twice a week, and I never go to bed before 10.
